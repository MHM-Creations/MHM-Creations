DON'T DROP THE PLATE!

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MHM Creations handles information in Don't Drop the Plate! and its related online services. We aim to collect and use only the information reasonably needed to operate, secure, support, and improve the game.

1. Information We May Process

  • Gameplay and run data: distance, score, servings, deliveries, obstacles hit, jumps, run timing, game version, and related validation information when a run is submitted.
  • Display name: if you choose to submit a name for leaderboards or supporter features. You may use an anonymous display name.
  • Technical identifiers: installation, session, run, and attempt identifiers and related security information used to authenticate sessions and prevent abuse.
  • Purchase information: for optional in-game support purchases, our services may process the Google Play product identifier, purchase token or related verification information, and supporter information needed to verify the purchase and provide its associated benefit. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Google Play Integrity information: for selected security-sensitive operations, the game may request a Google Play Integrity token. Our backend uses it to help verify that eligible requests originate from the recognized game and satisfy applicable integrity and licensing checks.
  • Support communications: if you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate game sessions, leaderboards, competitions, and supporter features.
  • Verify purchases and prevent fraudulent or duplicated purchase claims.
  • Protect competitive features against cheating, tampering, automated abuse, and invalid submissions.
  • Maintain service reliability, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support requests.
  • Improve the game and online services using operational or aggregated information where appropriate.

3. Google Play Services and Payments

Don't Drop the Plate! is distributed through Google Play. Optional purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google independently processes payment information under its own policies and terms. MHM Creations does not receive or control your full payment card information.

Google Play Integrity is used for selected security-sensitive requests. Integrity information is used for verification and anti-abuse purposes, not for advertising or profiling.

4. Service Providers

Our online game backend uses Appwrite Cloud for database, function, and service infrastructure. Information sent to our backend may therefore be processed by Appwrite to provide these services.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Don't Drop the Plate! does not use personalized advertising as part of the services covered by this policy. We do not use the information described here to build advertising profiles.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining leaderboard and supporter records, security information, resolving disputes, and preventing abuse. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and operational requirements.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can choose whether to provide a display name and whether to use optional supporter features. You may contact us with questions about personal information associated with your support request or online game activity, subject to reasonable verification and applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law, and we do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by our game services, including server-side validation and integrity checks for sensitive operations. However, no internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, our services, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact MHM Creations: support.mhmcreations@gmail.com
